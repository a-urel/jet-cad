# 08 — Openings: doors and windows

**Status:** not started
**Depends on:** 07 (hard)
**Blocks:** 10 (rooms must not be broken by doorways)
**Size:** M

---

## What this delivers

A door or a window hosted **in** a wall: it cuts the wall's faces, it moves
when the wall moves, it is positioned along the wall rather than in absolute
coordinates, and it draws a symbol — a swing arc for a door, a sill for a
window.

## Why it exists

It is the second half of what makes walls parametric rather than decorative.
It is also the **first real test of 06's dependency graph in the direction that
matters**: the wall's geometry depends on objects that are conceptually
children of it.

## What already exists

- 06's parametric mechanism and 07's walls. Nothing else is specific to this.
- `EntityKind.arc` — a door swing.
- `Definition` and `InstanceNode` — if opening symbols come from the library
  (09) rather than being generated.
- `segment_clip.dart` — for subtracting the opening from a face line.

## What does not exist

- Any hosting or containment relationship between objects.
- Any opening concept.

## Decisions already made

1. **An opening is a parametric object (06) that references its host wall.**
   `OpeningComponent` carries the host wall's handle, a position along the
   host's centreline, a width, and a type.
2. **Position is parametric along the host, not absolute.** `(handle,
   distance-along-centreline)` rather than `(x, y)`. This is what makes the
   opening move with the wall for free, and it is the whole point.
3. **The wall's regeneration takes its openings as input.** So the dependency
   edge runs *opening → wall*: editing an opening dirties its host. This is the
   inverse of the intuitive direction and it must be explicit in 06's graph.

## Open questions — these are the spec

- **Where does the symbol come from?** Generated by the opening's regeneration
  (self-contained, but every door looks the same), or an `InstanceNode` of a
  library definition placed by the opening (flexible, but couples 08 to 09 and
  makes 09 a hard dependency)? **Deciding this decides the roadmap order.**
- **`OpeningComponent`'s fields.** Host handle, position, width, type
  (door/window/opening), swing direction and hand for doors, sill and head
  height for windows.
- **What happens when the host wall is deleted?** Cascade-delete the openings,
  orphan them, or refuse the deletion? Cascade is conventional; it needs the
  compound command from 06.
- **What happens when the host wall is shortened past the opening?** Clamp,
  delete, or mark invalid?
- **An opening at or across a junction.** Two walls' worth of cutting, or
  forbidden?
- **Overlapping openings** in one wall — merge, reject, or allow?
- **Does dragging an opening along its wall snap** to wall ends, to other
  openings, to a grid?
- **Does an opening cut a poché fill** as well as face lines? If 07 chose
  fills, this is a boolean subtraction, which is substantially harder than
  trimming two lines. Price it before committing.

## Exit criteria sketch

- An opening cuts its host wall's faces at the documented position and width.
- Moving the host wall moves the opening with it, in one undo step.
- Rotating or lengthening the host keeps the opening at the same **relative**
  position.
- Deleting the host performs the documented cascade, in one undo step.
- Save → load → regenerate is byte-identical, with the host relationship
  intact.
- Two openings in one wall both cut correctly.

## Named mutants to fire

- **Required fixture properties:** the host wall must be **non-axis-aligned**;
  the opening must be at a **non-central** position along it (a centred opening
  in a symmetric wall hides sign errors in both directions); at least one wall
  must carry **two** openings; the plan sits at the corpus's far origin.
- **M-08a:** store the opening's position as absolute coordinates rather than
  as a distance along the host. The move-the-wall test must go red.
- **M-08b:** measure the position from the wrong end of the centreline. Must go
  red — **impossible with a centred opening.**
- **M-08c:** cut the opening at its centre point with zero width. A width test
  must go red.
- **M-08d:** dirty only the opening on edit, not the host wall. The wall's
  faces must fail to update — this is the dependency-direction mutant and it is
  the reason this sub-project exists as a test of 06.
- **M-08e:** cut only one of the two face lines. Must go red — **impossible if
  the test only ever inspects one face.**
- **M-08f:** on host deletion, delete the opening in a separate command. The
  one-undo-step test must go red.

## Traps

- **The dependency edge points the "wrong" way.** An opening is conceptually
  inside a wall, but the wall's geometry depends on the opening. If 06's graph
  only propagates from container to contained, this does not work.
- **A centred opening in a symmetric wall is this feature's degenerate
  fixture.** Sign errors, end-swaps and one-sided cuts all survive it.
- Poché fills turn "trim two lines" into "subtract from a polygon". If 07
  chose fills, do not discover this here.
- `Tolerance` for "is this position on the wall?"; `==` for stored positions.
