## R-13, amended: a mode switch keeps the plan in place (`98481c3`)

14b-2's R-13 kept the camera's *numbers* across a mode switch. The
camera's coordinates are the canvas's, and the editor's canvas starts
after its top bar, left panel and rulers (264, 68 in the view) while the
service's starts under its bar (0, 44), so the plan moved by (264, 24) at
every switch. The human ruled it should stay in place.

`FloorPlanView` listens to the mode and, when it changes, shifts the
camera by the difference of the two canvases' origins, its zoom kept.
The origins are seeded from the chrome's sizes (`floorPlanCanvasSeeds`),
so the first frame of the new mode is already in place; after that
frame the shown canvas's origin is measured (its interaction layer's top
left in the view) and kept, and a difference from the seed is corrected
at once. `fitToView` and `load` still fit. The controller is unchanged.
