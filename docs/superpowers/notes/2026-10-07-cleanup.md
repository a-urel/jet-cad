## R-13, amended: a mode switch keeps the plan in place (`98481c3`)

14b-2's R-13 kept the camera's *numbers* across a mode switch. The
camera's coordinates are the canvas's, and the editor's canvas starts
after its top bar, left panel and rulers (264, 68 in the view) while the
service's starts under its bar (0, 44), so the plan moved by (264, 24) at
every switch. The human ruled it should stay in place.

**The controller reframes the camera in `setMode`**, by the difference of
the two canvases' origins, its zoom kept — so a switch made while no
view is shown keeps the place too. The origins are seeded from the
chrome's sizes (`floorPlanCanvasSeeds`), so the first frame of the new
mode is already in place; each `FloorPlanView` measures, after the first
frame of each plan it shows, where that mode's canvas starts in it (its
interaction layer's top left) and reports it with the mode it built
(`canvasMeasured`). A reframing that assumed an origin the measurement
corrects is corrected with it: into the shown mode by what the
assumption missed, out of a mode no longer shown the other way.
`fitToView` and `load` still fit (the measurement runs before the new
view's fit, which wins).
