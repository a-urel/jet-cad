## (d) An orphan component (engine)

**Defect.** `SetComponentCommand<T>` for a registered parametric type, on a
handle that is not a live root-level group (dead, never allocated, nested),
attached a component the parametric layer never regenerates. It survived
save, load and purge, reported only as `parametric.misplaced`.

**Fix.** A guard in `_run`, after `inner` applies and beside the existing
`_refused` check: a touched handle on which `inner` **wrote** a registered
component (non-null and not `==` to what the before-survey held) and which is
not a live root-level group after `inner` is refused; `inner` is undone and
the edit throws `StateError`. "Wrote", not "carries": the literal rule
refused every delete (06 D8's cleanup runs later) and spec 08 D4's re-parent.
The before-survey keeps misplaced and shadowed components in a new
`_Survey.stray`, empty in the app's documents. Not in `SetComponentCommand`
itself: undo of a delete re-attaches before it restores the node (mutant
M-Q3e breaks exactly that undo). Spec 06 D5 and D6 amended.

**Not covered** (pre-existing): an edit 06 D2's fast path does not wrap, and
a command that under-reports `touched`. Loading still brings misplaced
components in; the diagnostic stays.

## (e) A wall-end drag gathers only live walls (app)

**Defect.** `wall_grips.dart`'s `_endsAt` gathered every `WallParams` in the
store. A file's orphan at the dragged joint moved along with the drag and
drew a phantom preview; with (d)'s guard, the drag's commit was refused and
the select tool's pointer-up rethrew it.

**Fix.** `_endsAt` and `gripsOf` take their walls from `wallsInDocument`, the
adapter the tools, grips and panel share, whose liveness is the engine's
root-level-group rule. EG6 drags a turned L's corner with three file-style
strays on it (a node-less handle, a nested group, a root-level instance); the
commit lands, the strays stay `==`, one undo restores everything. Every other
`withComponent` loop in the app was already filtered.

## (c) A wall thickness the panel accepted threw at turned placements (app)

**What the investigation found.** The Selection panel already caught the
refusal (the field reverted, nothing changed). The Wall tool did not: a
thickness typed without Enter, then a diagonal click, threw out of the pointer
handler, used up a handle per click and left the chain stuck. The band ring
collapses by precision only for an off-axis wall with thickness over length
around 1e16; the smallest thickness that threw in a sweep of 520,200 cases at
five placements was 1.33e10 mm.

**Fix.** `isWallThickness` has a ceiling, `kWallMaxThickness` = 1e7 mm
(10 km, a margin of about 1,300), refused by the panel and the Wall tool
exactly as the floor is. Spec 07 D11 amended. The review's sweep (142,560
cases at six placements, with the fixed triangulator) found no throw for any
thickness from 1e-3 mm to the ceiling. **Known limits:** a loaded wall
thicker than the ceiling still throws on the Wall tool's and grips'
regenerating edits; and the floor is absolute while the collapse is
relative, so at placements of 1e10 mm or more a wall of 2e-6 mm (1e-5 mm
from 1e11) still throws from the Wall tool (pre-existing).

## (B) The triangulator's winding decision far from the origin (engine, new)

**Defect.** `_signedArea` summed the shoelace on raw coordinates. Far from the
origin the rounding swamped the area of a thin ring: a 0.01 × 150 mm ring at
1e9 read −64.0 against 1.5, triangulated to nothing, and 07's region check
refused the edit. Through the Wall tool, walls up to about 3 mm long at
georeferenced millimetre coordinates were refused.

**Fix.** The sum is taken relative to the loop's first vertex. It is the
engine's only shoelace. Spec 3e's algorithm amended. **Debt:** the app's own
`signedArea` / `isSimpleCcw` (`wall_geometry.dart`) is still raw, so such a
far, short wall lands with a spurious `wall.fallback` (square ends); changing
it moves `WG10`, `WR12` and `KJ1` and is its own item.

## The panel's number text (app, new)

**Defect.** `_number` called `v.round()`, which saturates at 2^63 − 1: 1e20
showed as "9223372036854775807", and the focus-loss commit wrote a second,
silent undo step storing 9.22e18. A loaded infinite value threw.

**Fix.** `panelNumberText` shows a whole number below 2^53 as an integer and
anything else in Dart's round-trip form, so the text always parses back to
the stored value exactly (−0 included); a non-finite value shows as
"Infinity".
Its review found the same saturating formula in the page panel's scale field
(a scale of 1e20, then Enter on the unchanged text, wrote 9.22e18 as a step)
and in the status line; all three now share `panelNumberText`
(`lib/panel_number.dart`).

## Found, not fixed

- **Shadowing** (the Q1+Q2 review's m2, pre-existing): a root-level group
  carrying `WallParams` and a later-registered `OpeningParams` is an opening
  to the engine and a wall to the app's live-wall rule. Only a file makes it;
  with a dangling host, dragging a neighbouring wall throws
  `DanglingReferenceError`. The fix is one shared "which registration names
  the object" rule, the same refactor as the next item.
- **The root-level-group check is spelled five ways** in the app
  (`_keptPut`, `wall_bands`, two private `_isLiveGroup`s, `liveObjectsOf`).
  They agree today, each pinned by its own mutant.
- **06 D8's cleanup detaches only the naming registration**: an object
  carrying two registered types leaves the other component on the dead
  handle after a delete. No app path makes one.
- **The codec does not raise the handle seed for components**
  (`DraftDocumentCodec.decode`), so a hand-made file's component above the
  seed can be adopted by a later allocation.
- **The allocation case of fix/phantom-intersection-snap** still depends on
  its file's order (that branch's debt).
- **A −0.0 opening position shows "-0.0"** (a no-fit placement whose
  direction has two negative components): exact, but odd text.
- **The status line's zoom percentage** saturates at an absurd scale
  (display only).
- **A fractional page scale's text is unpinned** (the Q4b review's m1,
  pre-existing): every test pins an integer scale, so a change that rounds
  the scale before formatting (a 1:12.5 page showing "13", which Enter would
  write) survives the suite. The code is right today.
- Still open from 11: (b) a tapered piece under a scaled group (file only).
