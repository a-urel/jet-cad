Task 14's `opening_support.dart`, Task 18's `room_plans.dart` (wall names) and Task 26's `plans.dart` (wall and room names) import and re-export them instead of a further copy of the same source lines.
- **Order.** `room_tool_test` TT10 needs the dimension (Task 20); it moves from Task 19 to Task 20 with its two mutations (M-RT-7, M-RT-10).
