# Rooms and area (sub-project 10) — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** executed task by task, subagent-driven: a fresh
> implementer per task and an independent reviewer per task, then a final
> whole-branch review. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** rooms in the floor planner. A room is its own parametric object
that stores a seed point, a name and an optional label offset. It
regenerates from **every live wall and separator**: the face of the plan
that holds its seed, bounded by the walls' uncut inner faces and the
separators, minus the walls standing wholly inside it. It draws a
translucent tint on that face and two labels, its name and its net area in
the page's unit, at the face's pole of inaccessibility. It dissolves in the
same undo step when its seed ends up in a wall or in an unbounded face.

**What ships:**
- the Room tool (**M**, one click inside a face) and the Separator tool
  (**S**, two clicks);
- room separators: a free two-point line, drawn dashed, that splits a face
  as a zero-thickness wall would;
- a Room section in the Selection panel with a free-text **Name** field and
  the area; a label grip; end grips on a separator; a selected room
  outlines its labels **and its ring**;
- diagnostics: two rooms sharing one face, a broken room from a file, a
  tint that could not show its holes, degenerate rooms and separators;
- the sample plan with its seven rooms, the Living | Dining separator and a
  column.

**Architecture:**
- **The engine** (06's mechanism) gains, all generic:
  - (a) generated TEXT with its string (`Generated.text`), rewritten on a
    match;
  - (b) the record attributes a client sets on add (transparency, flags,
    boundary flags, linetype, lineweight, `textAttrs`);
  - (c) the page on `ParametricView`, and a per-type `pageKey` whose change
    seeds the type's objects;
  - (d) a per-type dissolve verdict, applied inside the edit, that removes
    the object and detaches its own component;
  - (e) spatial dependencies: the survey snapshots each object's component
    and transform (a true before-view), place **contributors** and place
    **readers**, a trigger that adds to the closure every reader whose read
    box touches the before or after box of a contributor whose input
    changed, an exact `placedIn` query with a bulk neighbour pass, and
    `objectsOf`;
  - (f) one reserved linetype handle, `ReservedHandles.dashedLinetype`.
- **The render layer** gains (g): a drawn fill whose boundary is not drawn
  outlines its boundary in the selection overlay, and the move and rotate
  preview leaves out keys the move leaves out.
- **The app** gains:
  - the trace inputs (uncut bands and separator segments, two adapters that
    agree bit for bit);
  - the tracer (a planar arrangement walked as half-edge faces), its
    localised form, the tint with keyholed holes, the pole of
    inaccessibility and the area format, all pure Dart;
  - `RoomParams`/`RoomType` and `SeparatorParams`/`SeparatorType`;
  - the two tools, the Room section, the label grip and the separator
    grips;
  - the sample plan's rooms.

## Rulings made here rather than left to an implementer

Each ruling says what the plan does, why, and what it costs if wrong.
Rulings marked **(spec amended)** are written into the spec in Task 20.

- **Ruling 10-1 — test identifiers and test files.** The spec's
  identifiers are kept ("the plan may renumber, keeping the kills"), with
  these changes:
  - **`SD10` is split.** Its first half (no live reader: no place-box call)
    stays `SD10`, in Task 6. Its second half (`objectsOf`) becomes **`OB1`**,
    in Task 7's `objects_of_test.dart`, the file the spec's Files section
    names for it.
  - **`RP2`** (the default tables unchanged) is an engine test, in Task 7:
    it pins D17, an engine property, and the engine suite is where the
    default document is built.
  - **The plan's own new tests:**
    - `SR5`: `ensureDashedLinetype`'s cases (D3's duplicate rules);
    - `DF1`: the spec's Differential check (from scratch against
      incremental, twenty edits);
    - `FZ1`: a seeded random run that no edit is refused because of rooms
      (08's final review found a refusal class only a random run could
      reach).

  10's app tests go into **new files** (`room_*_test.dart`,
  `separator*_test.dart`). **07's and 08's app test files are not edited**,
  with two exceptions: `startup_plan_test.dart` (Task 18, D23's tests) and,
  only if Task 18's measurement shows it is needed and the controller
  agrees, a sample-plan assertion in `planner_shell_test.dart` or
  `planner_draw_test.dart` (Ruling 10-22). Task 19 checks it with a diff.
  **Cost:** none.
- **Ruling 10-2 — the engine's test-only clients** (the spec's Files: a
  text client, a page-key client, a dissolving client, a contributor whose
  place box depends on its neighbours, a reader). They live in
  `packages/jet_cad_2d/test/parametric/support/clients.dart` and are
  registered in `testCatalog()`, except `SD8`'s two-role type, which is
  built into a catalog of its own inside its test. The names avoid every
  existing client (`ClipRect`, `SoftRect`, `Trip`, `Hinge`, `RegionRect`,
  `Post`, `Pin`, `Tag`, and the test-local `Peg`, `Brace`, `Mark`):
  - **`Caption`** (Task 1): a string and an insertion point; generates one
    `Generated.text` with `textAttrs` centre-middle;
  - **`Swatch`** (Task 2): generates a plain LINE, a region and a TEXT, each
    with non-default attributes read from its parameters;
  - **`Gauge`** and **`Dial`** (Task 3): page-key clients, keyed on the
    page's scale and on its unit respectively; each draws one LINE whose
    length reads its key; a Gauge may declare one `host` reference
    (`cascade`), which it otherwise ignores, for Ruling 10-4's case;
  - **`Fuse`** (Task 4): a rectangle that generates a region and a LINE, and
    dissolves when its `burnt` flag is set or when a `ClipRect` neighbour
    covers its centre (a verdict read from the after-view, as a room's is);
  - **`Slab`** (Task 5): a place contributor. Its reach is a 2 mm box at
    its first corner; its place box is its whole rectangle, grown by 1,000
    mm on every side while any neighbour is a `ClipRect`. So its place box
    differs from its reach (kills M-10cand) and depends on its neighbours
    at an end far from them (the two-hop shape, kills M-10nbr);
  - **`Rod`** (Tasks 5, 6): a place contributor that is a segment, with an
    `exact` flag: when set, its `placeInput` override returns the segment;
    otherwise it takes the default ("always changed", T-2);
  - **`Lens`** (Task 5): a place reader with a stored field box. `generate`
    emits, for each contributor `view.placedIn(field)` returns, the
    contributor's place box as a closed polyline, or a `Rod`'s segment as
    a LINE, world → own local. `readBox` is `stored` ∪ its world field,
    so an empty Lens still reads its field.

  Each client counts its `generate` calls per handle in the existing static
  `generateCalls` map, which tests reset. Registering them changes nothing
  for the existing tests: a page key, a dissolve verdict and the trigger
  act only on live objects of these types. **Cost:** none (07's Ruling 07-8:
  engine tests cannot import the app).
- **Ruling 10-3 — (e) lands in three tasks, staged.**
  - **Task 5** lands the place roles, the survey's snapshots (the true
    before-view), `placedIn` and `placeBoxOf` (a per-object neighbour
    search for now) and the trigger, with **every** contributor of `K`
    adding its boxes (revision 1's rule: it can only over-rebuild).
  - **Task 6** adds `placeInput`, the changed-input filter (S-4), the
    no-reader check and the two counters.
  - **Task 7** adds the bulk neighbour pass behind the first `placedIn`.

  **Why:** the before-view is read by nothing but the trigger, so
  `SV1`–`SV3` can only observe it through the trigger's `placeBox` calls.
  The before-view and the trigger therefore land together, and the rest is
  split off to keep each task one sitting. **Cost:** Task 5's tree
  over-rebuilds readers until Task 6; no drift is possible in between.
- **Ruling 10-4 — where the page seeds sit.** In `_run`, **after
  `_checkDangling` and before the early return**. The spec fixes only
  "before the early return" (S-12). The spike added them before the
  dangling check, which would let a loaded `cascade` referrer with a dead
  reference refuse every page change if its type reads the page: 08's rule
  is that "only seeds are checked, so an unrelated edit never trips over a
  bad object elsewhere", and a page-seeded object was not edited. **Cost:**
  none; `PG1` has the case.
- **Ruling 10-5 — one after-view per edit.** `_run` builds the after-view
  once, `ParametricView._(t, after)`, and hands the same object to the
  trigger and to `_plan` (S-12), so D4's band memo and the bulk pass are
  paid once per edit. The before-view is built only when the trigger runs.
  For a handle that is not a live object of its survey, the before-view's
  `toWorld` reads the live tree, as today (D16.1); no client asks that.
  **Cost:** none.
- **Ruling 10-6 — the place memo and the counters.** Each view keeps its
  place boxes in a map on the view object. `placeBoxOf(h)` asks the type
  once per view per handle; `placedIn` asks for every contributor on its
  first call. `_Registration.placeBoxOf` is the only caller of a type's
  `placeBox`: it increments `debugPlaceBoxCalls` and turns a box with a
  non-finite coordinate into null. A memo hit is not a call (T-4).
  `debugReadBoxCalls` counts calls into `readBox`, one site. Both are
  `@visibleForTesting` and never reset by the library. **Cost:** none.
- **Ruling 10-7 — the tracer's output is canonical.** `LZ1` asks the
  localised trace to equal the all-inputs trace **bit for bit**. The two
  arrangements hold the same near vertices, but the far inputs shift
  vertex indices, so without a rule the ring could start at another vertex
  and the shoelace sum would round differently. So:
  - the outer ring starts at its least vertex in `(x, y)` order in the
    seed-relative frame, anticlockwise; each hole likewise;
  - holes are ordered by their first vertex, in the same order;
  - sources move with their edges; areas are summed in that order;
  - `SeedInWall` names the **lowest-handle** input that holds the seed.

  The spec's D7 proof needs only "a function of `C` and the seed", which
  this keeps. **(spec amended**, D5.) **Cost:** a sort per ring.
- **Ruling 10-8 — the wall's local ring, shared.** 07's `_localOutlineOf`
  (`wall.dart:144`) becomes a thin wrapper over a new pure
  `localOutlineOf(WorldWall self, List<WorldWall> neighbours)` in
  `wall_geometry.dart`, so the room's two adapters and the wall's own
  generate compute one ring (R-5). The wall's stored children are
  unchanged bit for bit: 07's and 08's tests pass unedited. **Cost:** none.
- **Ruling 10-9 — where the pure pieces live.**
  - `roomTrace` (R-6) is declared in `room_inputs.dart`, because the
    inputs need it first (a separator no longer than `roomTrace.linear` is
    degenerate, D3); the tracer imports it.
  - `tintOf` (D9) is in `room_trace.dart`: it works in the tracer's
    seed-relative frame.
  - The pole of inaccessibility (D10) and `formatArea` (D11) are in
    `room_label.dart`.
  - `trimSeparator` (D20's band trimming) is in `room_inputs.dart`, **one
    function** that the Separator tool and the separator grips both call,
    so M-10trim has one site.

  All four files are pure Dart. **Cost:** none.
- **Ruling 10-10 — the document adapter's neighbour predicate.** D4 asks
  the document adapter to find a wall's neighbours "by the engine's own
  predicate (reach overlap by more than `Tolerance.standard.linear` on both
  axes)". `room_inputs.dart` therefore holds **one** use of
  `Tolerance.standard.linear`, in a named constant whose comment says it is
  the engine's neighbour predicate, not a geometric decision of rooms.
  Task 19's `Tolerance.standard` grep expects exactly that one line in the
  room files. Reaches come from `WallType().reach` itself. **Cost:** none;
  `RI1` would turn red on any drift from the engine's lists.
- **Ruling 10-11 — one room-input cache in the shell.**
  `RoomInputs` (`room_inputs.dart`) is the document adapter: every live
  wall's band and every live separator's segment, their place boxes and
  the bounding box `U` of the finite ones, marked stale on each
  `doc.changes` event and rebuilt at the next query. It holds **live
  objects only** (a root-level `GroupNode` carrying the component: 08's
  final review m5 found a cache that did not). The shell owns one instance
  and passes it to the Room tool, the Separator tool and `SeparatorGrips`.
  It is not 08's `WallBands`, which caches straight strips for joining,
  not 07's outline. **Cost:** none.
- **Ruling 10-12 — how `RI1` sees the view adapter.** A `ParametricView`
  exists only inside `generate` and `diagnose`. `RI1` builds a test-only
  catalog (Wall, Opening, Separator and a test-local `_Probe` type) and a
  `ParametricSystem` over the fixture document; one `_Probe` object's
  `diagnose` records `roomInputInView(view, h)` and `view.neighbours(h)`
  for every contributor. They are compared with `RoomInputs(doc)` with
  `==`. **Cost:** none.
- **Ruling 10-13 — `U` for the view source.** D7's growth stops when `B`
  contains `U`, the union of every contributor's finite place box. The view
  has no "all contributors" query, so the view source computes `U` once per
  view as the union of `placeBoxOf(h)` over `placedIn(box)` with an
  infinite box, memoised in the room's per-view memo. **Cost:** none: the
  first `placedIn` already computes every box.
- **Ruling 10-14 — two literals the spec leaves open.**
  - The labels' justification, "centre, middle", is
    `packTextAttrs(h: TextJustifyH.centre, v: TextJustifyV.middle)`. Not
    `TextJustifyH.middle`, which is DXF's other "middle".
  - `LinetypeRecord` requires a description. The DASHED record's is
    `'Dashed __ __ __'`.

  **Cost:** none.
- **Ruling 10-15 — placeholders** (08-7's precedent).
  `SeparatorType.diagnose` and `RoomType.diagnose` return `const []` until
  Task 13. Every task still ends green. **Cost:** none.
- **Ruling 10-16 — how `RG2` reaches fallback step 2 in a room.** A traced
  outer ring is simple by construction, and the slit keyhole triangulates
  on every fixture the spike tried, so no honest room fixture is known to
  take step 2. `room.dart` gains `@visibleForTesting Set<int>?
  debugTintFailedSteps` (null in the library, never set by it): the room's
  tint builder treats each listed step (1 or 2) as failing to triangulate.
  Tests restore it to null in `tearDown`. `RG2`'s step-2
  case sets it and asserts what D9 says: step 2's region, the room alive,
  its area label unchanged, `room.tint` reported. M-10slit is still killed
  by `RG2`'s **column** case, which runs without the hook and must take
  step 1. If Task 13's implementer finds an honest step-2 fixture (a column
  within 0.5 mm of a face is the candidate), it replaces the hook and the
  hook goes. **Cost:** one test-only seam in `room.dart`.
- **Ruling 10-17 — the label grip's return to auto is not gated on F3.**
  R-26 makes "a drop within the snap aperture of the pole" the one way back
  to auto. `RoomGrips` takes `double Function() aperture` from the shell,
  `kSnapAperturePixels / _camera.value.scale`, **whatever F3 says**: it is
  a reset gesture, not a snap, and with F3 off it would otherwise be
  unreachable. **Cost if wrong:** with object snap off, a label dropped on
  its pole returns to auto.
- **Ruling 10-18 — "the hover allocates nothing" is read as counters**
  (08-12's precedent). Dart has no allocation counter. `TT6` pins that a
  hover inside the cached face neither traces (`debugTracedSegments`
  unchanged) nor builds a preview (`RoomTool.debugPreviewBuilds`
  unchanged), and that the overlay paints the cached payloads. The render
  layer's allocation invariants stay green and unedited. **Cost:** none.
- **Ruling 10-19 — `RK2`'s room count.** `room.dart` keeps
  `@visibleForTesting int debugRoomGenerates`, incremented by every
  `RoomType.generate` call and never reset by the library; `RK2` prints

- **Ruling 10-20 — the sample plan's numbers are measured, then pinned**

- **Ruling 10-21 — the Differential check's fixture.** The spec asks for

## Global constraints

- **Pure-Dart engine.** Nothing under `packages/jet_cad_2d` imports
  Flutter or `dart:ui`. `apps/floor_planner/lib/parametric/wall_geometry.dart`,
  `opening_geometry.dart`, `room_trace.dart`, `room_label.dart` and
  `room_inputs.dart` import no Flutter either.
- **The frame path allocates nothing per entity in steady state, and O(1)
  per flush.** `packages/jet_cad_2d/test/invariants/query_allocation_test.dart`
  and `packages/jet_cad_2d_flutter/test/invariants/paint_allocation_test.dart`
  stay green **and unedited**. Traces, poles and previews run per edit or
  per pointer move, never per frame. The outline cache (D24) is built at
  selection, hover and `DocChange` rate; per frame the preview filter is
  one set lookup per selected key.
- **Draw order is ascending handle value,** stable across undo, save, load
  and purge. A room's children are reserved in generation order, tint fill
  < tint boundary < name < area, and keep their handles for its life
